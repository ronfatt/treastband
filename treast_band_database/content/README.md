# TREAST BAND — INTERNATIONAL WEBSITE DATABASE

## Positioning
**The Sound of Semporna**  
Born in Semporna. Rooted in culture. Made for the world.

Treast should be presented as an independent Malaysian rock band and creative music collective from Semporna, Sabah — not as a generic event/wedding band. The site should combine a four-member core band, featured vocalists, original releases and a professional production layer.

## Recommended website story
1. **Home** — cinematic hero; immediate music/video playback.
2. **Our Story** — Semporna roots, brotherhood, local language/culture, original music.
3. **The Band** — four core musicians first.
4. **Artists** — featured singers/collaborators.
5. **Music** — releases, videos, credits, streaming links.
6. **Productions** — songwriting, arrangement, production, MV and branded music.
7. **Live & Booking** — stage formats and booking enquiry.
8. **Contact** — management / collaboration / media.

## Visual direction
- Premium international artist-site feel.
- Dark cinematic palette, warm skin tones, deep blacks, restrained gold/amber accents.
- Tropical/Borneo atmosphere through texture, wind, sea haze, tree shadow and documentary footage — **do not turn it into a tourism website**.
- Oversized editorial typography, full-bleed 16:9 video, smooth scroll, subtle parallax.
- Mobile-first.
- Featured releases should look like international label campaign pages.
- Avoid template-looking cards everywhere; use editorial layouts and cinematic transitions.
