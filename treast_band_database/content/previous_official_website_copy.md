# 02 — INTRODUCTION

### Section headline

FOUR MUSICIANS. ONE SHARED SOUND.

### Copy

Treast Band is an independent rock band from Semporna, Sabah, formed through family spirit, friendship and a shared belief in original music. At our core are four musicians who have grown together on stage and in the studio, shaping a sound that is unmistakably our own.

Our music carries the intensity of rock, the warmth of regional melodies and the emotional character of Sabah. We sing about love, longing, celebration, identity and everyday life — the stories that stay with people long after the final note.

Treast is more than a performing band. Together with our featured singers, songwriters, producers and filmmakers, we are building a creative home where local voices can be heard, developed and shared with a wider world.

### Pull quote

“We do not copy where music is going. We create from where we come from.”

# 03 — OUR STORY

### Section headline

FROM SEMPORNA, WITH SOMETHING REAL TO SAY

### Copy

Treast began with a simple connection: musicians coming together, playing from the heart and discovering a sound that felt honest. What started as a shared passion became a long-term commitment to write, perform and produce music on our own terms.

Semporna is often known for its islands and sea. To us, it is also a place filled with voices, languages, memories and rhythms. This cultural environment continues to shape how we write and how we see the world. Our roots are local, but the emotions inside our songs are universal.

Over the years, Treast has expanded beyond the four members on stage. We collaborate with singers from across Sabah, develop original releases, produce music videos and create music for events, brands and personal stories. Every collaboration adds a new voice without losing the identity at the centre of Treast.

Today, we remain proudly independent — building one song, one artist and one audience at a time.

### Closing line

This is music from home, made to travel.

# 15 — EDITORIAL DIRECTION

- Use “Treast Band” for formal introductions and “Treast” after the first mention.
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as “No. 1”, “world-class” or “internationally acclaimed” unless evidence is available.
- Write from a confident independent-artist perspective, not like a wedding entertainment supplier.
- Keep Semporna visible throughout the story, but do not turn the website into tourism advertising.
- Present the four core members first, featured singers second, and producers as a separate creative leadership layer.
- Use short headlines and restrained body copy on animated sections so the scroll experience remains cinematic.
